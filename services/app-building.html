What this is good for

Not every business needs a full software platform. Sometimes you just need one clean place that tracks leads, clients, quotes, invoices, and follow-ups without five tabs open at once.

  • You have a spreadsheet that three people are editing and nobody trusts anymore
  • Leads and follow-ups are getting lost in texts, emails, and sticky notes
  • You want to see where all your active jobs stand without asking someone
  • Your quoting or invoicing process only makes sense to you and one other person
  • You run assessments or audits and want a tool that walks through them cleanly
  • You are collecting numbers from different places but never have them in one view
  • You need something simpler than a full CRM but more organized than a shared doc
  • You have an internal process that should not live in a public app or shared folder

What I do not overbuild

The first version is focused on solving the actual problem. Once you are using it and know what is missing, we improve it from there.

  • No long feature list that turns a two-week job into a six-month project
  • No subscriptions or third-party platforms bolted on unless the job genuinely needs them
  • No database if a simpler setup handles the job just as well
  • No fancy UI just to make it look impressive — clean and usable is the goal
  • Hosted locally, on a VPS, or as a static site depending on what the build actually requires

What is included

  • Written scope before any build starts
  • A working first version built around your actual process, not a generic template
  • Plain-English handoff documentation
  • Basic input handling and secure defaults
  • Deployment to a local server, VPS, or static host depending on the build
  • A walkthrough session so you can use it and ask questions before it is handed off
We keep the scope clear before any build starts. Hardware, licenses, cloud bills, travel, and after-hours work are separate unless the signed Statement of Work says otherwise.

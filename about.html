About EPIC TECH

Technology built for small businesses. Security-first, documented, and priced to fit

EPIC TECH LLC was built to help small businesses get the type of clean, documented technology setup that usually gets ignored until something breaks.

How we work

We focus on clear scope, simple communication, and systems that can be handed off. That means no confusing technical smoke, no mystery pricing, and no pretending a small business needs enterprise-level complexity on day one.

The goal is to build a setup that is safer, easier to manage, and easier to understand.

What we believe

  • Security should be built in from the start.
  • Documentation matters.
  • Small businesses deserve clear pricing.
  • Not every problem needs a giant platform.
  • The right first step is usually an assessment.
Founder

Ethan Platt

Ethan is the founder of EPIC TECH LLC and a former United States Marine who worked as a communications and transmission systems operator. That background is where the habit of documenting everything and testing before trusting a system came from.

He holds a bachelor's degree in Information Technology and a bachelor's degree in Cybersecurity, and lives in the Orlando area.

Most small businesses cannot justify hiring a full IT department, but they still end up running on whatever network setup or website got thrown together years ago. Ethan started EPIC TECH so those businesses would have someone to call who scopes the work honestly and writes the plan down before anything changes.

When you work with EPIC TECH, you are talking to Ethan directly, not a sales team or a rotating support queue. If something breaks after handoff, you call the person who built it.

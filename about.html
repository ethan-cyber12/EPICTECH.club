About EPIC TECH

Small business IT run by the person who does the work

EPIC TECH LLC is a one-person shop. When you call, you are talking to the same person who sets up your firewall, builds your website, or writes your app. No account manager, no hand-off to a stranger.

Founder

Ethan Platt

Ethan is the founder of EPIC TECH LLC and a former United States Marine who worked as a communications and transmission systems operator. That background is where the habit of documenting everything and testing before trusting a system came from.

He holds a bachelor's degree in Information Technology and a bachelor's degree in Cybersecurity, and lives in the Orlando area.

EPIC TECH exists because most small businesses cannot justify hiring a full IT department, but still end up running on whatever network setup or website got thrown together years ago. Ethan started the company to give those businesses a straightforward way to fix that: one person, a clear scope, and a written plan before anything changes.

What to expect

  • You talk directly to Ethan, not a sales team or a rotating support queue.
  • Every project starts with a written scope and price before work begins.
  • Nothing gets built bigger than the problem actually requires.
  • You get the setup notes and documentation when the project wraps up, not just a bill.
  • If something breaks after handoff, you call the person who built it.

Common questions

What areas does EPIC TECH serve?

EPIC TECH LLC serves small businesses in Orlando, Winter Park, Maitland, Oviedo, Lake Mary, and the wider Central Florida area, on-site and remotely.

What is a technology assessment?

A technology assessment is a paid review of your network, website, firewall, or IT setup. You get a plain-English written plan with exact scope and pricing before any larger project begins.

Do I need an ongoing IT contract?

No. Most small businesses start with a single assessment or project. Monthly care plans are optional and only added if ongoing support makes sense for you.
